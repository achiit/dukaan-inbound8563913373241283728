Is planner ko aise use karo

  1. Goal page bharo. 5 minute lagenge. Wajah sach-sach likho.
  2. Apna timetable banao. Tumhara din, tumhare rules.
  3. Chapter tracker tick karo. Har chapter ke stages poore karo.
  4. Roz ka Today page bharo. Raat ko 10 minute.
  5. Mock ke baad mistake notebook. Jo galti likhi, wo dobara nahi hogi.

Countdown roadmap

176 days. Six missions. One rank.

November: Build the Base

  • NCERT Biology Class 11: line by line, underline every fact
  • Physics + Chemistry Class 11: theory first, then numericals
  • Tick Theory in the tracker, start the mistake notebook
  • Test: 1 chapter or subject test every week

December: Class 11 Done

  • Finish every pending Class 11 chapter
  • NCERT Biology: solve every in-text and exercise question
  • Chapter-wise PYQs for each finished chapter
  • Mock: 1 full mock every week

January: Class 12 Begins

  • Class 12 Biology: Genetics, Reproduction, Ecology first
  • Physics + Chemistry Class 12 theory and numericals
  • Revise Class 11 weak chapters twice a week
  • Mock: 1 full mock every week

Physics tip

Mechanics (Ch 2 to 7) is the foundation of NEET. Concept first, then numericals. Do not skip Units.

Botany tip

Read NCERT line by line. Learn the diagrams and tables. Cell (Ch 8 to 10) is common with Zoology.

Zoology tip

Human Physiology (Ch 14 to 19) matters most. Keep a list of classification exceptions and revise it every 3 days.
